How to read the result

  • Incumbent stress predicts whether the White House party is politically vulnerable.
  • Electoral conversion models geography: popular support can be inefficiently distributed.
  • Democratic conversion risk models breakdowns: recount fights, ballot rejection, court intervention, suppression, fake electors, certification sabotage, or violence.

Why this differs from 13 Keys

Lichtman gives one point per key that turns against the incumbent party. This model keeps that incumbent-party logic but adds lived material stress, legitimacy/moral injury, conversion geography, and democratic machinery risk.
